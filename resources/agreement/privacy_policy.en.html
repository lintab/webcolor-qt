KQ Cloud — Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the KQ Cloud desktop client ("the Software") collects, how it is stored and used, and what control you have.

1. Information we collect

  1. Account information: username, password (used only to sign you in), phone number or e-mail (registration and verification codes), company name, province, brand, gender and supplier — the details you enter when registering or editing your profile.
  2. Measurement data: spectral reflectance, colour values per observation angle, lightness and chroma, metallic flake and grain, texture and colour-chip images captured through a connected colorimeter (for example SDX4), together with the measurement jobs you name.
  3. Work data: search criteria, correction and formula-prediction results, formulas you download or save, and your search history.
  4. Runtime information: the version number of the installed Software, read only to check whether an update is available.

2. What is stored on your machine

  1. The session credential (token) and your user profile are kept in the Windows registry under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\KaiqiChemical. The credential is deleted automatically when the Software closes, so the next start requires a new sign-in.
  2. Preferences such as interface language, number of measurements per job, colour-chip display mode, formula save path, remembered account and search history are stored in the same key so your settings are restored next time.
  3. Downloaded formula files and update installers are written to the folder you choose or to the program default folder.

3. How the information is used

  1. To identify you, enforce permissions and show the colourant, formula and vehicle data of your company.
  2. To run colour search, matching, colour correction and formula prediction, and to compare measurements with the standard panel.
  3. For team sharing: your measurement jobs and own formulas can be seen by other authorised accounts of the same company.
  4. For update checks and troubleshooting.

4. Transfer and sharing

  1. The data above is sent over encrypted HTTPS to the Software's own backend at color.kaiqichemical.com, which exists solely to serve this Software.
  2. We do not sell, rent or otherwise disclose your personal or work data to third parties.
  3. Except where disclosure is required by law or by a competent authority.

5. Protection

Transport uses HTTPS and every request carries your session credential, which the server validates for identity and permissions. Keep your password safe and sign out on shared computers.

6. Your rights

  1. You can review and edit your account details under Profile.
  2. You can ask the system administrator to inspect, correct or delete data related to you.
  3. Uninstalling the Software and removing the registry key above deletes the locally stored credential and preferences.

7. Changes to this policy

Updated versions are published with new releases and shown in this dialog. Continuing to use the Software means you accept the updated policy.

8. Scope

The Software is an in-house production and colour-matching tool for companies; it is not directed at minors and does not knowingly collect their data.
